Accessibility

Last updated October 18, 2026

We aim to make our website usable for people with a broad range of abilities and assistive technologies. We design for readable content, clear navigation, keyboard operation, semantic document structure, and reduced-motion preferences.

Help and feedback

If you experience a barrier, email contact@potluck.software with the page address and a description of the issue. We welcome suggestions for improving accessibility.

Scope

This statement covers the Potluck Labs corporate website. Each product may have distinct interfaces and accessibility information. This statement is not a claim of certification or an independently audited conformance level.
